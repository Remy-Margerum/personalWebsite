The House

A family winery with an Italian heart

Santi Rosina began the way most good things do — around a table. We grow Italian varietals in the hills of Santa Barbara County, where morning fog and afternoon sun do quiet, patient work, and we make wine the way our family always has: unhurried, honest, and meant to be shared.

The cellar is small on purpose. The list is short on purpose. Everything else is the land's doing.

— the family at Santi Rosina
