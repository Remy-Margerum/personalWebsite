Fetching the forecast…

Start, finish, and gate legs run to the middle of the G–F and F–X lines. Bearings magnetic (var 12.5°E); published distances from the club chart. The forecast is sampled at every mark — the small arrows on the chart and the wind column show the local wind for the hour you pick above, and the ★ sits on whichever end of the start line that wind favors. Hover a buoy for its coordinates.
